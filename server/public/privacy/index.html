Privacy Policy

Application: Hi Talk - Telecalling CRM (Package: com.teleapp.crm)
Developer: Hi Talk
Effective Date: October 22, 2026

At Hi Talk, we are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our users and enterprise customers. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and web-based telecalling CRM services.

1. Overview & Purpose of the Application

Hi Talk is an enterprise customer relationship management (CRM) and sales outbound telecalling tool designed exclusively for businesses, telecalling teams, and sales representatives. The application enables telecallers to manage assigned leads, perform structured telephone outreach, automatically log call metrics, and update lead disposition stages efficiently.

2. Sensitive Android Permissions & Why We Need Them

Prominent Disclosure: Hi Talk only accesses sensitive telephony features to fulfill its core functionality as an enterprise sales dialer and call logging tool. We never sell, rent, or distribute personal call logs or private contact information to third parties.

🔹 android.permission.READ_CALL_LOG

Purpose: As an enterprise telecalling application, Hi Talk requests access to device call logs strictly to measure call outcomes, timestamp logs, and accurately calculate the talk-time duration of phone calls made to assigned business leads. This enables automatic lead pipeline status updates (e.g., Connected, Busy, Not Lifted) and schedules automated retry callbacks without requiring tedious manual agent entry.

🔹 android.permission.CALL_PHONE

Purpose: Allows telecallers to initiate phone calls to designated leads directly from the lead card interface with a single tap, eliminating manual number typing errors and accelerating outreach velocity.

🔹 android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE & READ_PHONE_NUMBERS

Purpose: Used to detect telephony call state transitions (e.g., call started, call active, call disconnected) so the application can immediately trigger the post-call disposition modal and calculate accurate call durations.

🔹 android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Purpose: Used to deliver timely push notifications to agents regarding scheduled lead follow-ups, overdue callbacks, and new lead assignments made by managers.

3. Information We Collect

In operating the Hi Talk CRM platform, we process the following types of information:

  • Account & Agent Information: Telecaller name, official email address, mobile number, role (Agent, Manager, Admin), and organization affiliation provided during account registration.
  • Lead Data: Contact details (names, phone numbers, campaign tags, notes, and pipeline stages) uploaded by authorized organization managers or admins into the CRM.
  • Call Performance Metrics: Call timestamps, durations, disposition labels, and follow-up schedules created during agent outreach.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and network connectivity state for application diagnostics, crash prevention, and security verification.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and optimize CRM and telecalling operations.
  • To synchronize lead updates and call activity between the mobile application and desktop web dashboard in real time.
  • To provide managers and administrators with objective performance reports (daily calls, talk-time, conversion rates).
  • To enforce security controls, prevent unauthorized access, and protect system integrity.

5. Data Security & Encryption

Security is our utmost priority. All communications between the Hi Talk mobile application, web dashboard, and backend database are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Database records are stored within protected, access-controlled virtual private server infrastructure with strict role-based authorization.

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or monetize personal user or lead data. Data is only accessible to authorized personnel within your registered organization (e.g., your account Administrator or Team Manager). We may utilize trusted infrastructure providers (such as Expo EAS for application packaging and cloud hosting providers) strictly governed by confidentiality agreements.

7. Data Retention & User Rights

Organization data is retained as long as the organization maintains an active subscription or account. Users and administrators have the right to:

  • Access, view, and export their registered CRM leads and activity logs.
  • Correct or update inaccurate lead and agent records.
  • Request Account & Data Deletion: Any organization or individual can request the permanent deletion of their account, leads, and call logs by contacting us directly at support@hitalk.app. Deletion requests are fulfilled within 30 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Hi Talk is strictly designed for enterprise business use by working professionals and is not directed at or intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
